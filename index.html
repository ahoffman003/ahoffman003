Overview

I am a GIS Analyst that loves solving real-world problems with spatial data. I’m especially drawn to projects that blend environmental insight, spatial data science, and intuitive cartography. Across all my work, I’m driven by clear communication, thoughtful design, and building tools that help people understand and make decisions about the world around them. I currently work as a GIS Analyst at Apex Engineering Group supporting municipal infrastructure projects. My work includes utility mapping, asset managment, exhibit creation, Survey123 design, and Experience Builder tool development to help interal staff and cities leverage spatial data in their day-to-day operations.
